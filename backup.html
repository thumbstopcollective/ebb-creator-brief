✅ Dos / Don'ts

✅ REQUIRED

  • Clear CTAs. Show visual proof. Before / After. Show curls, volume, comb through knots, shine in the sun.
  • Use product up close. Show texture. Not just packaging. Flip the video so words or logos are not backwards.
  • Open with the problem: post-swim knots, frizz, heat damage.
  • Share your hair story: hair type, struggles, routine.
  • Add subtitles. Most people watch on mute. Use simple native fonts. Keep it centered.
  • Use text overlays, zooms, sound fx in hook.
  • Film in natural light. Hair clearly visible.
  • Keep videos 20 to 50 seconds.
  • Say Ebb is available at EbbOceanClub.com and Sephora

❌ DON'Ts

  • Over-explain the science. Show, don't tell.
  • Stage the product like an ad.
  • Sound scripted or sales-y.
  • Make medical or guaranteed claims ("regrows hair").
  • Call it sunscreen or say it has SPF.
  • Bash competitors by name.
