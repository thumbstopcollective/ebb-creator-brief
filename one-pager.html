What is Ebb?

Hair care inspired by life in and around the water, made for sun, heat, hard water and pollution.

  • Your hair has a barrier, just like your skin. Ebb is made to care for it.
  • Made with seaweed, sea moss and sea kelp
  • Reef safe and made with recycled ocean plastic

Must include

Three things every video needs.

1

Your frizz, then Ebb

Show your frizz, then how Ebb helps, in your own words.

2

Where Ebb fits in your routine

Say which step it is and when you use it: daily, weekly or before heat.

3

Before and after, side by side

Same light, same angle, hair moving.

Do

  • Name your hair type and how your frizz shows up
  • Film in daylight so the frizz and shine show
  • Talk like you’re FaceTiming a friend
  • Say Ebb is available at EbbOceanClub.com and Sephora

Don’t

  • Promise hair growth, scalp fixes or “frizz gone forever”
  • Say Ebb is “better than” another brand
  • Open on a discount, or film only the bottle with no face
  • Set the product up like a staged ad

Before you post

  • Subtitles on, centered
  • Hook text on screen in the first second
  • A clear ending: available at EbbOceanClub.com and Sephora
  • Your frizz and your Ebb routine on camera
  • @ebboceanclub tagged on Instagram and TikTok
  • Partnership request accepted

Talking points

Feature a full routine. Pick the one that fits your hair.

DailyShampoo, conditioner, hair oil
WeeklyShampoo, conditioner, hair mask (your at-home hair spa night)
Heat + sunLeave-in spray first, then hair oil
  • ShampooWashes off environmental pollutants
  • ConditionerSoftens and nourishes
  • Hair MaskGives frizz-prone hair a deep, soft reset
  • Leave-In SprayGoes on first, before hot tools or a day in the sun
  • Hair OilLocks in hydration and protects from styling tools and daily UV sun damage

Pick a format

Choose the one that feels easiest. The must-haves stay the same.

A

Frizz confessions

The moment your frizz always shows up, then your routine taking it on. Try a get-ready-with-me or your hair from morning to night.

B

Everything shower

Your whole shower, start to finish. Try a shower-shelf tour or a talk-through as you wash.

C

Pre-style + heat style

Leave-in spray, hair oil, then your hot tools. Do half your head with Ebb, then check both sides later. Bonus: take it out in the sun.

D

Beach/pool day essentials

What you bring for a day by the water. Try a pack-with-me or a sit-down chat about your Ebb favorites.
