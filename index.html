02

Your video

Three things every video needs, then what to do and what to skip.

1

Frizz in the first frame

Show your frizz and say how it happens for you, in your own words. Hook first, brand second.

2

Ebb in your routine

Say which step it is and when you use it: daily, weekly or before heat.

3

The proof on camera

Before and after, hair in motion. Show it, don’t claim it.

Do

  • +Name your hair type and how your frizz shows up
  • +Film in daylight so frizz and shine really show
  • +Talk like you’re texting a friend, not reading an ad
  • +Add subtitles. Most people watch on mute
  • +Flip the video so the label isn’t backwards

Don’t

  • −Promise growth, scalp fixes or “frizz gone forever”
  • −Say Ebb is “better than” another brand
  • −Open on a discount or film only the bottle, no face
  • −Stage the product like an ad

Talking points

Feature a routine, not one product. Pick the one that fits your hair, then use the prompts below to find your own words.

Start with you

  • “My frizz shows up when ____.”
  • “I’ve dealt with ____ for years, so here’s my routine.”
  • “Wash day with me. Frizz included.”

Say these points

  • Barrier care for frizz-prone hair
  • Made with seaweed, sea moss and sea kelp
  • Reef safe, with recycled ocean plastic

Wrap up like a friend

  • “If I could only keep one step, it’d be ____.”
  • “How does your frizz show up? Tell me.”
  • “That’s my routine. What’s yours?”
