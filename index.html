What is Ebb?

From Sea to Shower. Inspired by life in and around the water, Ebb applies that same awareness to hair care. Our formulas are designed to perform under stress—from UV exposure, heat styling, harsh or hard water, pollution, and product buildup—so hair stays resilient wherever life takes you.

The idea

Your hair has a barrier, just like your skin, and Ebb is made to care for it.

The fix

Barrier care powered by seaweed, sea moss and sea kelp. Reef safe, made with recycled ocean plastic.

02

Must include

Three things every video needs.

1

Problem-solution

Show your frizz, then how Ebb fixes it for you, in your own words. Hook first, brand second.

2

How Ebb fits in your routine

Say which step it is and when you use it: daily, weekly or before heat.

3

Before & after, side by side clips

Same light, same angle, hair in motion. Show it, don’t claim it.

Do

  • +Name your hair type and how your frizz shows up
  • +Film in daylight so frizz and shine really show
  • +Talk like you’re texting a friend, not reading an ad
  • +Add subtitles. Most people watch on mute

Don’t

  • −Promise growth, scalp fixes or “frizz gone forever”
  • −Say Ebb is “better than” another brand
  • −Open on a discount or film only the bottle, no face
  • −Stage the product like an ad

04

Pick a format

Format A30–60 sec

Frizz confessions 💇‍♀️

Spill the moment your frizz always shows up (your ____ day, your ____ plans), then show your routine taking it on.

Ideas: a get-ready-with-me, a voiceover over your worst frizz clips, or your hair from morning to night.

Format B30–60 sec

Everything shower 🚿

Film your whole shower, start to finish: shampoo, conditioner, mask, everything you use in there.

Ideas: a shower-shelf tour, a talk-through as you wash, or a rundown of everything you use in there.

Format C30–60 sec

Pre-style + heat style 🔥

Film your prep before the heat: leave-in spray, hair oil, then your hot tools. Do half your head with Ebb and half without, then show both sides again later in the day. Bonus: take it out in the sun.

Ideas: a split-head blowout or curling iron session, a morning-to-night check-in, or a golden-hour clip in direct sun.

Format D30–60 sec

Beach/pool day essentials ⛱️

Show what you bring on a beach/pool day, or sit down and talk through your Ebb favorites, in your own words.

Ideas: a pack-with-me, a bag tour, or a sit-down chat about your top picks.
